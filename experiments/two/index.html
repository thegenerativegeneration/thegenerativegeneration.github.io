Voice Puppetry (2023)

A mannequin audio visualization.

This visualization uses audio input to let 3D mannequins dance. I am planning to expand on this idea to let attendees of a 3D installation move through it with just their voice. (Does not work with Firefox.)


The model file is from BlenderKit
